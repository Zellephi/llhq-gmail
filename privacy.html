Privacy policy

LLHQ Gmail. Last updated 28 October 2026.

Who uses this app

LLHQ Gmail is a private tool used only by its owner, Liezel Loyola, with her own Google accounts. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, the app reads Gmail messages, threads and labels in read-only mode. It cannot send, change or delete email.

How the information is used

Email content is fetched on request so the owner's AI assistant can search and summarise her own email for her. Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer.

Sharing

Information from Google accounts is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

LLHQ Gmail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
